The community

Ivory Homes markets Broadview Shores to first time buyers and move up buyers alike, with a mix of cottage style homes and larger two story floor plans. The development covers roughly 250 acres in its current phase, with room to grow toward 500, and includes single family homes with some multifamily and commercial planned over time.

Lifestyle and amenities

  • Community park and pickleball courts
  • Community pool and clubhouse planned
  • Walking distance to Lakeview Park
  • Utah Lake boating, paddleboarding and State Park nearby
  • Golf at Sleepy Ridge and Riverside Country Club
  • Provo River and hiking trails close by

Location and convenience

  • 2 minutes to Provo High School
  • 5 minutes to I 15 and Provo Airport
  • Minutes to BYU, UVU and University Place Mall
  • Costco and Revere Health Hospital nearby
  • Close to Orem
